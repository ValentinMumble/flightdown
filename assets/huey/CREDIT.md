# Huey model

`Huey2.0.fbx` is the low-poly Huey from [Helicopter](https://samifd3f122.itch.io/helicopter) by
[Sami](https://samifd3f122.itch.io), © 28 Nov 2022, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Only the helicopter is kept: its weapons, textures and Unity
package are left out.

`npx tsx scripts/huey.ts` turns it into `src/components/charts/huey.json`, the damage map's 3D heli: its parts sorted
into the damage zones, the body cut straight across into canopy, frame, boom and tail, and a main motor added inside.
